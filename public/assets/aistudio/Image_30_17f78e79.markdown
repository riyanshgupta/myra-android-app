## Brand & Style
The design system bridges personal acoustic immersion with high-visibility automotive utility. Designed for seamless operation across both Android handheld devices and Android 10 automotive head units, the interface prioritizes glanceability, high contrast, and tactile confidence while preserving a sleek, atmospheric, late-night aesthetic.

The visual style is **Tactile Glass & Luminescent Dark**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep spatial tones eliminate cockpit glare and display burnout during nighttime drives while offering an intimate listening lounge experience on mobile.
- **Instrument Precision:** Crisp, technical vector lines and luminescent accents simulate high-end automotive instrument clusters mixed with modern software synthesis tools.
- **Restrained Glassmorphism:** Performance-conscious translucent fills with micro-borders replace compute-heavy live backdrop blurs, ensuring sustained 60fps rendering even on low-tier automotive SoCs running Android 10.

## Colors
The palette leverages high-contrast luminescence over a multi-tiered midnight canvas.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0A0D14`):** True deep-space charcoal base preventing OLED bleed and distraction during driving.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0F1420`):** Secondary canvas, side rails, bottom navigation containers.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#161C2A`):** Standard interactive cards, mini-player vessel, list groupings.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#1E2638`):** Elevated overlays, floating controls, dialogs, active pills.
- **Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6` / `#7C3AED`):** Primary brand driver, AI active states, primary interactive triggers.
- **Radiant Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Ambient accent, playback progress track, gradient terminations.
- **Luminous Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Secondary voice feedback, streaming quality badges, active EQ indicators.
- **Soft Rose (`#F43F5E`):** Expressive actions, favorites, hearts, dynamic passion metrics.

### Contrast & Boundaries
- Surface borders utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on default containers, escalating to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.40)` on active or voice-responsive elements.
- Text hierarchy conforms to strict automotive readability standards: Primary (`#FFFFFF`), Secondary (`#94A3B8`), and Micro/Metadata (`#64748B`).

## Typography
The typography strategy prioritizes optical clarity under sub-optimal viewing angles, such as an arm's-length automotive dashboard in daylight or a handheld screen under direct sun.

- **Primary Typeface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its wide aperture, geometric clarity, and humanist touches, avoiding visual congestion in dense playlists.
- **Data & Micro UI (`Inter`):** Applied to labels, technical tags (FLAC, 96kHz, Dolby Atmos), timestamps, and HUD readouts for maximum optical density and crisp pixel fitting.
- **Driving Legibility Rules:** Any typography displayed on head unit screens during active transit must not fall below `14px` (`body-md`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements an adaptive dual-canvas architecture engineered for two distinct ergonomic contexts: Vertical Handheld and Horizontal In-Car Cockpit.

### Spatial Ergonomics
- **Handheld Canvas (360dp - 430dp):** Fluid single-column layout. 16px lateral margin, 16px gutters. Single-handed thumb zones govern the lower 40% of the display.
- **Automotive Canvas (800dp - 1280dp wide landscape, 7-10 inch head units):** 2-column or 3-column split interface. 32px safety margins avoiding bezel intrusion. Minimum touch target boundaries expand to `56dp x 56dp` (up from `48dp x 48dp` on handheld).

### Safe-Zone Guardrails
- **Floating Bar Offsets:** All content lists must include a dynamic scroll padding equivalent to `mini_player_height (64px) + bottom_nav_height (64px) + 32px` to prevent occlusion.
- **Hardware Bezel Compliance:** Car dashboards commonly feature raised bezels; outer bounds enforce `margin-car: 2rem` to prevent clipped taps.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed structurally without relying on runtime-intensive raster blurs, ensuring fluid framerates on low-memory vehicle systems.

### Tonal Stratification
1. **Level 0 (Floor):** Pure `#0A0D14`. Background canvas.
2. **Level 1 (Dock & Side Panel):** `#0F1420` with a 1px top or right border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Cards & Modules):** Translucent composite `#161C2A` (92% opacity) wrapped in a crisp 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Floats & Mini-Player):** `#1E2638` (96% opacity) with a dual boundary: 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` exterior, and a directional top rim light of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`.

### Chromatic Glows
Interactive audio and voice states utilize localized emissive shadows:
- **Audio Active Glow:** `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`.
- **AI Listening Pulse:** Radial ambient wash `0px 0px 40px 8px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.
- **Liked Affirmation:** `0px 4px 16px 0px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.30)`.

## Shapes
A balanced geometric corner profile (`16px / 1rem` standard base) offers modern sophistication while cleanly framing dense media covers.

- **Album Artwork & Media Cards:** Set strictly to `16px` (`rounded-lg`) corner curvature to maintain visual consistency across aspect ratios.
- **HUD & Command Pills:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) styling for tags, state badges, voice command chips, and quick action filters.
- **Mini-Player Container:** `16px` curvature when floating on handheld screens, shifting to `0px` bottom radius when docked flush against car unit display bottoms.
- **Touch-Target Outlines:** Inner controls (play/pause, skip) live inside fully circular (`9999px`) protective tap bounding boxes.

## Components

### Sleek Glassmorphic Cards
- **Construction:** Background `#161C2A` with 90% opacity, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **States:** Hover/Touch focused scales smoothly to 1.02 with border shifting to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5)`.
- **Album Grid:** High-fidelity cover art fills with top inner-vignette to ensure readability of floating text tags.

### Mini-Player Bar
- **Dimensions:** 64px fixed height. Floats 12px above the bottom navigation rail or sits flush in landscape auto mode.
- **Styling:** `#1E2638` with 94% opacity, bounded by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Elements:** 44px album art miniature (8px radius), Marquee Track Title, Artist, Voice Assistant invocation icon, and large touch-area Play/Pause toggle (48dp x 48dp minimum). Integrated progress bar exists as a 2px track along the very top edge (`#8B5CF6`).

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Styling:** Dark midnight surface (`#0F1420`) with 64px structural height.
- **Active State:** Glowing pill highlight featuring an Electric Violet tinted base (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`) with an accented active indicator and high-contrast white icon.

### AI Assistant HUD
- **Listening Orb:** Radial gradient core shifting from `#8B5CF6` to `#06B6D4` with a breathing scale animation between 0.95 and 1.05.
- **Waveform Interface:** 5-bar to 16-bar vertical parametric equalizers rendered in `#06B6D4` with smooth amplitude decay.
- **HUD State Pills:** Pill containers displaying contextual text (e.g., "Finding upbeat driving jazz...", "Playing daily commute radar") with an illuminated outer boundary and micro-indicator beacon.

### Quick Action Pills & Chips
- **Resting:** `#161C2A` surface, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border, 32px height, 14px horizontal padding.
- **Selected:** Gradient wash from `#7C3AED` to `#6366F1`, zero border, `display-color: #FFFFFF`, accompanied by an active glow.

### Lists & Track Rows
- **Metrics:** 64px row height on mobile, 72px on car units for driver glanceability.
- **Content:** Track index/EQ visualizer, 48px square art asset, Title in `#FFFFFF`, Subtitle in `#94A3B8`, right-aligned duration in `#64748B`, and overflow button with a 48px tap envelope.
- **Divider:** Recessed divider inset by 72px using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
